1. Introduction

Al Ihsan Foundation ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, register for the Weekly Scheme, or make a donation.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to process your membership, deliver receipts, and maintain transparency. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name (or Alias for Privacy Members), email address, and phone number.
  • Location Data: Physical address (collected only for General Membership applications).
  • Financial Data: Transaction reference numbers from bKash or Bank Transfers. (Note: We do not store, see, or process your bKash PINs, bank passwords, or full card numbers).
  • Technical Data: Basic device and browser information automatically collected when you browse the site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To verify your identity using One-Time Passwords (OTP) and secure your membership account.
  • To maintain internal records of weekly contributions and donations.
  • To send automated real-time email receipts and monthly newsletters.
  • To respond to your inquiries, complaints, or requests for financial transparency.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. This includes:

  • Using Supabase for secure authentication (passwords and OTPs are encrypted).
  • Utilizing JSON Web Tokens (JWT) to securely verify form submissions and block malicious bot traffic.
  • Storing internal records on secure Google Workspace (Google Sheets) platforms with restricted access.

5. Third-Party Services [Members Only]

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data of registered members with trusted third-party platforms:

  • Supabase: For secure user authentication and OTP generation.
  • Brevo: For delivering transactional emails (notifications, OTPs, and newsletters).
  • Google (Apps Script & Sheets): For automated backend processing and internal record-keeping.
  • Make: For Automated email workflow process.
  • Zappier: For Automated Membership Application Process.
  • n8n [self host]: For Advance Automation Workflows.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you remain an active member of the Weekly Scheme, or as long as necessary to comply with our legal and charitable accounting obligations. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal identifiers, though anonymized financial records may be kept for audit purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to any inaccurate information.
  • Request that your email be removed from our newsletter list at any time.
  • Withdraw from the Weekly Scheme (though past donations remain non-refundable as stated in our Terms).

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal, essential browser storage to keep you logged in during your session and to remember your form preferences. We do not use aggressive third-party tracking or advertising pixels.
