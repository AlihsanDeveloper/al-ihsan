What We Do

Our Core Operations

Weekly Scheme

We collect consistent weekly contributions from our members and direct them to carefully selected, verified candidates facing medical or educational emergencies.

Community Welfare

We invest in our local infrastructure by supporting mosques and community centers with the resources, maintenance, and financial aid they need to thrive.

Ramadan Campaign

Our largest annual program, uniting members and donors to distribute massive-scale grocery packages to families across the region before Ramadan begins.
