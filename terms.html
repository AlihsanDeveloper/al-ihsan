1. Introduction & Organisational Values

Preamble: Al Ihsan is a charitable organisation guided by the principles of Islamic finance. In order to preserve the integrity of our operations and ensure alignment with our core values, the following policies and terms govern all member and donor participation.

Values: Al Ihsan's activities are deeply rooted in the ethics of Islamic finance, particularly inspired by the spirit of Sadaqah. All members and donors are expected to respect and uphold the Islamic values that shape our initiatives.

2. Membership Eligibility & Conduct

Participation in the Al Ihsan Weekly Scheme is open to individuals of sound moral character who respect the principles of Al Ihsan. The organisation reserves the right to accept, suspend, or terminate any membership found to be in breach of conduct or posing reputational or operational risk.

Members must refrain from actions or speech that may disrupt organisational harmony or violate Islamic charitable ethics.

3. Weekly Contribution Requirements

Members shall contribute 10 Taka per week, consistently and sincerely, as an act of voluntary charity. These funds are pooled to support verified emergency cases and our flagship programs.

4. Nature and Finality of Donations

Donations made to Al Ihsan (either through the Weekly Scheme or one-time gifts) are strictly charitable, irrevocable, and non-refundable. Members and donors shall not claim, demand, or attempt to withdraw any donated amount under any circumstance.

While we strive to allocate funds to specific campaigns (e.g., Ramadan), Al Ihsan reserves the right to redirect funds to where the need is most urgent and impactful if a specific campaign is overfunded or cancelled.

5. Authority & Governance

All operational, financial, and programmatic decisions rest solely with the Al Ihsan corporate governance body. Members and donors shall not interfere with or challenge organisational authority, nor directly influence fund allocation.

Constructive feedback may be submitted formally and will be reviewed at the discretion of the board.

6. Financial Transparency

Al Ihsan is committed to transparent accounting practices. Annual financial summaries and impact reports are made available upon request to reinforce public trust.

7. Data Privacy & Communication

Member information is handled with strict confidentiality and care, used solely for organisational purposes. All official communication will be conducted through designated and approved channels (such as automated Brevo emails).

We use secure third-party services (like Supabase for authentication and Google Sheets for internal record-keeping). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

8. Prohibited Activities

Members and users of this website must not:

  • Misrepresent the organisation in any form, online or offline.
  • Use Al Ihsan's name, logo, or assets for personal or political gain.
  • Disrupt or obstruct our operational activities, digital platforms, or events.

9. Voluntary Withdrawal

Members may exit the Weekly Scheme at any point, effective upon written notice to the administrative committee. Withdrawal does not entitle the member to reclaim previously donated funds.

10. Dispute Resolution

Any grievance or concern must be submitted in writing to our administrative committee via email. The committee's decision shall be final and binding, in accordance with the charitable mission and Islamic ethics.
