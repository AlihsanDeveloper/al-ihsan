Preamble
Al Ihsan is a charitable organisation guided by the principles of Islamic finance. In order to preserve the integrity of our operations and ensure alignment with our core values, the following policies and terms govern all member participation.

Organisational Values
Al Ihsan's activities are deeply rooted in the ethics of Islamic finance, particularly inspired by the spirit of Sadaqah. All members are expected to respect and uphold the Islamic values that shape our initiatives.

Membership Eligibility & Conduct
Participation is open to individuals of sound moral character who respect the principles of Al Ihsan. The organisation reserves the right to accept, suspend, or terminate any membership found to be in breach of conduct or posing reputational or operational risk. Members must refrain from actions or speech that may disrupt organisational harmony or violate Islamic charitable ethics.

Contribution Requirements
Members shall contribute 10 per week, consistently and sincerely, as an act of voluntary charity.

Authority & Governance
All operational, financial, and programmatic decisions rest solely with the Al Ihsan corporate governance body. Members shall not interfere with or challenge organisational authority, nor influence fund allocation. Constructive feedback may be submitted formally and will be reviewed at the discretion of the board.

Nature and Finality of Donations
Donations made under this scheme are charitable, irrevocable, and nonrefundable. Members shall not claim, demand, or attempt to withdraw any donated amount, under any circumstance.

Financial Transparency
Al Ihsan is committed to transparent accounting practices. Annual financial summaries and impact reports shall be made available upon request to reinforce public trust.

Data Privacy & Communication
Member information shall be handled with confidentiality and care, used solely for organisational purposes. All official communication will be conducted through designated and approved channels.

Dispute Resolution
Any grievance or concern must be submitted in writing to the administrative committee. The committee's decision shall be final and binding, in accordance with the charitable mission and Islamic ethics.

Prohibited Activities
Members must not: Misrepresent the organisation in any form, online or offline; Use Al Ihsan's name for personal or political gain; Disrupt or obstruct operational activities or events.

Voluntary Withdrawal
Members may exit the scheme at any point, effective upon written notice. Withdrawal does not entitle the member to reclaim previously donated funds.
