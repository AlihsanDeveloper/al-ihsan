Al Ihsan is committed to maintaining the highest standards of conduct, transparency, and Islamic ethics. We recognize that despite our best efforts, concerns or disputes may occasionally arise. This policy outlines how we handle such matters fairly, promptly, and with dignity.

1. Our Commitment to Fairness

We take all complaints seriously. We believe that constructive feedback and open communication are essential to improving our services and maintaining the trust of our members, donors, and beneficiaries. No individual will be penalized or treated unfairly for submitting a complaint in good faith.

2. Scope of Complaints

This policy applies to grievances related to the following areas:

  • Financial Matters: Concerns regarding fund allocation, missing donation receipts, or questions about our transparency reports.
  • Conduct: Reports of unprofessional behavior, misconduct, or unethical actions by any Al Ihsan team member, volunteer, or representative.
  • Data Privacy: Concerns regarding the mishandling or unauthorized sharing of personal information.
  • Operational Issues: Technical failures regarding membership registration or the distribution of aid.

Please note: As stated in our Terms & Conditions, donations are final and non-refundable. A request for a refund does not qualify as a valid complaint under this policy.

3. How to Submit a Complaint

To ensure a proper record is kept, all complaints must be submitted in writing. Verbal complaints cannot be formally processed. Please send your grievance to:

Email: alihsanfoundation219@gmail.com
Subject Line: Formal Complaint - [Brief Subject]

To help us investigate promptly, please include the following details in your email:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • The date, time, and location of the incident.
  • The names of any Al Ihsan individuals involved (if applicable).
  • A clear, detailed description of the issue and any supporting evidence (receipts, screenshots).

4. The Resolution Process

Once a written complaint is received, the following steps will occur:

  • Acknowledgment (2-3 Business Days): We will send you an email confirming that we have received your complaint and are reviewing it.
  • Investigation: Your complaint will be escalated to the Al Ihsan Administrative Committee. We may contact you for further clarification or interview other parties involved.
  • Resolution (14-30 Days): We will provide a final written response detailing our findings and any corrective actions taken. Complex cases may require slightly more time, but we will keep you updated.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation

All complaints will be handled with strict confidentiality. Information will only be shared with committee members who need to know in order to resolve the issue. Al Ihsan strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against an individual for filing a complaint in good faith.

6. Finality of Decisions

Our goal is always an amicable resolution based on justice and Islamic ethics. However, as outlined in our Terms & Conditions, the final decision of the Administrative Committee shall be considered final and binding.
