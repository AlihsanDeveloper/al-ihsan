At Al Ihsan, we believe that trust is the foundation of charity. When you donate to us, you are placing a sacred trust in our hands. This page outlines our commitment to financial integrity, Shariah compliance, and absolute transparency.

1. Our Commitment to Transparency

We are committed to ensuring that every Taka (৳) donated to Al Ihsan is accounted for and used strictly for its intended charitable purpose. We operate with a 100% Transparency Policy, meaning we do not hide fund flows, and we openly share our financial data with our members and donors.

2. How Funds Are Allocated

To maintain clarity, we segregate our operations into distinct pillars. Funds donated for a specific purpose are strictly ring-fenced:

  • Weekly Scheme: Contributions from members (10 Taka/week) are pooled to support verified emergency medical cases, educational needs, and day-to-day operations.
  • Ramadan Campaign: Funds raised during our flagship campaign are used exclusively for the purchase and distribution of grocery packages.
  • Community Welfare: General donations or specific appeals directed towards mosque renovations, water wells, or local infrastructure.

If a specific campaign is fully funded, any surplus may be redirected to the most urgent emergency fund at the discretion of the committee, as outlined in our Terms & Conditions.

3. Zakah Policy & Shariah Compliance

Al Ihsan takes the handling of Zakah funds with the utmost religious responsibility:

  • Strict Segregation: Zakah funds are kept entirely separate from Sadaqah (voluntary charity) and general operating funds.
  • Eligibility Verification: Zakah is only distributed to individuals who strictly meet the Shariah criteria of Zakah-eligibility (e.g., the poor, the needy, the debt-ridden).
  • No Administrative Overhead: Whenever possible, administrative costs are covered by Sadaqah or member contributions, ensuring 100% of your Zakah reaches the eligible recipient.

4. Administrative Costs

We are a lean, member-powered organisation. Our team operates on a voluntary basis or with minimal stipends. Any technical or administrative overheads (such as web hosting, domain renewal, or bank transfer fees) are kept to an absolute minimum and are clearly documented in our internal ledgers.

5. Reporting & Evidence

We don't just promise transparency; we provide evidence. We maintain detailed digital records for every campaign.

For our major campaigns (such as Ramadan), we publish comprehensive PDF Reports that detail the number of packages distributed, families helped, and the exact expenses incurred. You can download these directly from our Campaigns Page.

6. Requesting Financial Records

While we publish campaign-specific data, we understand that donors may want a broader picture. Active members and donors have the right to request a high-level annual financial summary.
